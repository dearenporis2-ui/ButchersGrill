Our Story

Born in the
Islands

Before there was a KFC or McDonald's in Seychelles, there was us. What started as a tiny outlet at Mont Fleuri — chips, sausages, burgers — struck such a nerve with locals that a chain was born.

As a sister brand of Fresh-Cuts, Seychelles' most trusted meat retailer, quality has always been in our DNA. Today we serve everything from flame-grilled classics to authentic Creole dishes. Affordable, fresh, made with heart.
